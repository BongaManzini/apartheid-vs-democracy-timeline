## About This Project

In heated political arguments online, people sometimes claim that modern policies like BEE or employment equity are 'just apartheid in reverse'.

As an Informatics and information systems student, I wanted to compare the two eras directly using the actual legal texts. Apartheid laws literally criminalized where people could walk, live, work, and marry under threat of jail and police beatings. Democratic redress laws under Section 9 of our Bill of Rights are voluntary business development incentives with zero criminal penalties.

This tool puts both eras side by side so anyone can see the historical and legal differences for themselves.

## The 11 Questions: The Facts Behind The Project

### 1. Who is this for?
- High school and university students, educators, and anyone wanting a clear factual comparison between apartheid legislation and constitutional redress.

### 2. Where does this focus on?
- South Africa.

### 3. Why did I build it?
- To clear up the confusion between racial oppression laws and voluntary constitutional equality programs.

### 4. How does the timeline work?
- A split-screen interactive slider where you can scroll through the decades.
- On the left: Apartheid statutes (Group Areas Act, Bantu Education, Pass Laws).
- On the right: Democratic constitutional rights and redress legislation.

### 5. When did these laws apply?
- Compares 1948–1990 (Apartheid) with 1994–2026 (Constitutional Democracy).

### 6. The Problem I Noticed
- Younger South Africans who did not live through apartheid sometimes get confused by false claims made on social media.

### 7. Where I Got The Data
- Historical statutes from the South African state archives and the official 1996 South African Constitution.

### 8. Why I Trusted This Source
- Primary legislative acts are the only reliable way to compare legal penalties and constitutional rights.

### 9. How I Built It
- I compiled key acts from both eras, categorized their legal mechanisms (criminal jail penalty vs voluntary economic incentive), and built an interactive dual-axis comparison UI.

### 10. What I Hope To Achieve
- Help young South Africans understand our constitutional history through direct legal evidence.

### 11. Why This Matters In Real Life
- Visual comparisons make complex legal history easy to understand for everyone, not just lawyers.
