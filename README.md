# 🇿🇦 Apartheid vs Democracy Dual Timeline

> **Live Application**: [https://bongamanzini.github.io/apartheid-vs-democracy-timeline/](https://bongamanzini.github.io/apartheid-vs-democracy-timeline/)

An interactive dual-axis legal informatics comparison contrasting Apartheid legislation (1948–1990) with South African Constitutional Democratic legislation (1994–2026) to dissect the false narrative that *"BEE is reverse Apartheid."*

---

## 🎯 The Informatics & Civic Verification Angle

- **The False Equivalence**: Online rhetoric frequently describes post-apartheid redress frameworks (BEE, affirmative action) as an inverted version of Apartheid racial discrimination.
- **The Legal Reality**:
  - **Apartheid System (1948–1990)**: Operates through **criminal penal sanctions, forced evictions, pass arrests, and outright bans** on black South Africans acquiring property, entering cities, or learning advanced science/mathematics (Bantu Education Act).
  - **Democratic Redress (1994–2026)**: Operates through **non-penal commercial scoring, skills tax rebates, and voluntary procurement incentives** (Section 9(2) Equality Clause). Private non-transformed companies operate freely with zero criminal sanction.

---

## 🛠️ Features

- **Side-by-Side Thematic Comparison**:
  - Employment & Labor (Job Reservation Act 1956 vs Employment Equity Act 1998)
  - Land & Housing (Group Areas Act 1950 vs Restitution of Land Rights Act 1994)
  - Education & Skills (Bantu Education Act 1953 vs Skills Development Act 1998 & NSFAS)
  - Capital Accumulation (Physical Planning Act 1967 vs B-BBEE Act 2003)
- **Domain Filter**: Rapidly isolates specific areas of legal and socio-economic transformation.
